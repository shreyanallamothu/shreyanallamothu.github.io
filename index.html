About Shreya

Shreya Nallamothu is a software engineer, product builder, and technology policy advocate. From building AI-powered products at startups to authoring landmark technology legislation, she is passionate about creating technology that expands opportunity while protecting the people it serves. Her work spans machine learning, full-stack software engineering, product development, and public-interest technology.

Shreya is grateful to be a Robertson Scholar, National Merit Scholar, Coolidge Scholar, and U.S. Senate Youth Program Scholar. She is currently pursuing dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Statistics at Duke University as a Robertson Scholar, where she is also involved with Duke Impact Investing, Campus Enterprises, Carolina App Team, and Tech for Change. Previously, Shreya authored and passed the nation's first law protecting child influencers. She is passionate about building human-centered technology and shaping policies that ensure innovation benefits everyone.
