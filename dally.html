Here’s how to set it up

  2. 1 Pick your object Stick an NFC tag onto whatever you want to use as your blocker. Download the free Foqos app and create a profile. Choose the apps you want to block, then scan your NFC tag to link it to the profile.

  3. 2 Create an automation Open the Shortcuts app, tap Automation, then tap + to start a new one.

  4. 3 Connect your tag Choose NFC, tap Scan, and hold your phone near the tag to register it. Give it a name, then tap Next.

  5. 4 Add the Foqos action Select Foqos as the action, choose the profile you just made, and turn off “Ask Before Running” so it triggers instantly. Tap Done.

Have access to a 3D printer? Use my STL files!

I designed this pause button in Tinkercad to fit a 25 × 25 mm NFC sticker and 5 × 2.8 mm magnets. In your slicer, add a pause around layer 43, just before the print starts covering the slot. Drop the NFC sticker into the slot, then resume printing to seal it inside. Once the print is done, glue the magnets into the holes on the underside.

If your NFC sticker is a different size, feel free to resize the slot in Tinkercad!
